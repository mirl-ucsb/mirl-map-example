How to use this map

Map navigation

  • Drag to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Click any photo marker to open it in the side drawer.
  • Hover a marker for a brief tooltip and its sightline.

Top bar

  • Map and Essay: the two readings of the same photographs.
  • Streets, Satellite, Topo: switch the basemap.
  • Search: search captions, file names, and narratives (/).
  • Wander: open a random photograph (W).
  • Aa: text size, high contrast, and reduced motion.

Layers

Toggle the optional overlays you have turned on in js/config.js (landmarks, sightlines, area polygons, and so on).

Photo drawer

  • Use Prev and Next, or the arrow keys.
  • Cite this photograph: a citation in Chicago, MLA, APA, or BibTeX.
  • The address updates with ?photo=…, a permalink you can share.

Export

A portable index of every photo and overlay. Each placemark links back to this map.
